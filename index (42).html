The Missing Tier: One Argument for a Dedicated Naval Layer Against Attack Drones at Sea
An analysis by (retired) Rear Admiral Hasan Özyurt — who works at ULAQ Global, a maker of armed unmanned surface vessels — argues that cheap one-way attack drones form a middle tier between air defence and C-UAS that navies address poorly. Firing costly interceptors at ~$20-50k drones drains the defender faster than the attacker; at sea, shore systems detect low-signature drones only within ~10km, leaving minutes or seconds. His fix: unmanned surface vessels pushed 30km forward with AESA radar and low-cost effectors, plus force-level C2. The framework is widely discussed — though readers should weigh the author's commercial interest.
